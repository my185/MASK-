How To Work?

Follow these simple steps to start auto leveling.

1

Create a Free Fire Guest Account

Open Free Fire on your phone. If you don't have an account, create a Guest account.

2

Sign Up / Login Here

Click Get Start → choose Sign Up or Login. Only for your dashboard access.

3

Click "Add Bot"

Top-right → click + Add Bot. Two tabs: Guest Account and Access Token.

4

Fill in the Details

Enter your Free Fire UID + Password, select region, then Validate & Start.

5

Auto Play Starts

Bot auto-searches matches and plays Lone Wolf or Battle Royale, gaining EXP automatically.

6

Monitor & Control

Use the toggle button per account to Pause (🔴) or Resume (🟢), and set EXP limit for auto-delete.
